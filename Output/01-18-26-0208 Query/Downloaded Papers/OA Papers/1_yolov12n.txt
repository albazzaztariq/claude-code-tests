Developing fabric-based electronics with good wearability is undoubtedly an urgent demand
for wearable technologies. Although the state-of-the-art fabric-based wearable devices have
 ﬁ shown unique advantages in the eld of e-textiles, further efforts should be made before
 “ ” achieving electronic clothing  due to the hard challenge of optimally unifying both promising
ﬁ electrical performance and comfortability in single device. Here, we report an all- ber tribo-
ferroelectric synergistic e-textile with outstanding thermal-moisture comfortability. Owing to
ﬁ a tribo-ferroelectric synergistic effect introduced by ferroelectric polymer nano bers, the
  − 2 maximum peak power density of the e-textile reaches 5.2 W m under low frequency
motion, which is 7 times that of the state-of-the-art breathable triboelectric textiles. Elec-
ﬁ tronic nano ber materials form hierarchical networks in the e-textile hence lead to moisture
wicking, which contributes to outstanding thermal-moisture comfortability of the e-textile.
ﬁ The all- ber electronics is reliable in complicated real-life situation. Therefore, it is an idea
prototypical example for electronic clothing.
