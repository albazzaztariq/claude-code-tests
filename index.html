The Problem

Cotton Was Never Built for This.

Cotton robes absorb sweat and hold it. By your second match they're heavy, clingy, and cold against your skin. You take it off and it stays wet. You put it back on and it chills you. That's not a robe doing its job — that's a wet rag.

We built something different. A robe that moves moisture away from your body instead of trapping it — so you can cycle through a full tournament feeling the same after match six as you did after warmup.
