Usage Guide

On. Off. On Again.

The Titan X robe is engineered around one reality: wrestlers cycle through warmup, wait, compete, wait, compete again. Here is exactly how to use it.

🔥
Moment 01

Before the Match

You've warmed up. Your muscles are primed, your body temperature is elevated. This is exactly when you need the robe — not after you cool down.

Slip it on immediately after your warmup routine. The nanofiber layer begins active moisture management from the first second of contact, pulling sweat away while the robe's insulation preserves your elevated core temperature until your name is called.

Muscles stay warm and loose
Sweat moves away, not against skin
You step onto the mat as ready as you were off it
⚡
Moment 02

Between Matches

This is where most robes fail you. Cotton gets heavier with every match. By the time you have three or four bouts in a day, it's a wet, clingy drag that chills you when you put it back on.

The Titan X robe dries fast enough to be put back on between matches without that clammy feeling. Take it off to compete. Put it back on to recover. Repeat — without the performance degradation of a soaked-through cotton robe.

Dries during the time you're on the mat
No heavy, clingy return feeling
Consistent warm-up between every bout
Stay tournament-ready across a full day
🌬️
Moment 03

Post-Session Cool-Down

After practice or your final match, your body temperature begins to drop. This is when cotton is at its worst — cold, wet, held against your skin.

The Titan X robe handles the cool-down phase by continuing to move moisture away as your body equilibrates. You don't get the chilled, clammy feeling that cotton creates when your heat output drops and the absorbed sweat turns cold against your skin.

Manages the temperature drop phase
No post-session chill from wet fabric
Comfortable through the full session arc
The Key Difference

It Doesn't Hold. It Moves.

The distinction between cotton and nanofiber comes down to a single principle: absorption vs. transport.

Cotton is a natural absorber. When sweat hits cotton, it's taken in and held. That's fine for a towel. For a robe you cycle on and off during competition, it means you're effectively wearing a saturated sponge by match three.

The Titan X robe doesn't absorb sweat. It moves it — through capillary force in the nanofiber membrane — from the inner face toward the outer face, where it can evaporate. The moisture is never stored inside the fabric. It passes through.

This is why you can take it on and off between matches without that clammy, clingy weight that tells your body it's time to stop performing.

Care Guide

Keeping It at Peak Performance

The nanofiber membrane requires straightforward care to preserve its structure and function.

🫧

Machine Wash Cold

Gentle cycle. Cold water preserves nanofiber membrane structure.

🚫

No Fabric Softener

Softeners coat the nanofiber pores and reduce moisture transport performance.

💨

Hang Dry Preferred

Air-dry or low-heat tumble. High heat can damage the nanofiber membrane.

🔥

Do Not Iron

Never iron the nanofiber layer. The sub-micron fiber structure is heat-sensitive.

Why These Rules Matter

The electrospun nanofiber membrane performs through its physical structure — billions of microscopic pores working together. Fabric softeners physically coat those pores and block them. High heat can melt or distort the sub-micron fibers. Following these simple guidelines preserves the performance that makes this robe different from everything else.
