What's Customizable

Every Detail, Your Way

The Titan X robe is made to order. Here is what you can specify.

Robe Color

Choose the base color for your robe. Red and blue are shown in the examples, but we can produce in any color you need — for your national team, your club colors, or your own style.

  • Red, Blue, Navy, Black standard
  • White, Green, Gold available
  • Custom Pantone / hex on request
  • Solid or two-tone options

Back Text

Your name. Your country. Your team. Your club. Any text you choose is printed across the back in bold block lettering — the same clean style seen on the "ABDELFATAH" and "USA" examples.

  • Athlete name or surname
  • Country or nationality
  • Team or club name
  • Any custom text

Back Graphic

Below the text, you can add a flag, a national crest, a team logo, or any other graphic. The USA flag example shows how crisp and large these prints can be — this is not embroidery, it's full-color precision printing.

  • National flag
  • Team or club logo
  • National crest or emblem
  • Custom graphic (provide file)

The Process

How to Place a Custom Order

Four steps from inquiry to robe.

01

Contact Us

Use the contact form or email us directly. Tell us the quantity, color, and what you want on the back.

02

Submit Your Design

Send us your text, flag, logo, or any graphic file. We'll prepare a mock-up for your approval.

03

Confirm & Produce

Approve the mock-up and we go into production. Lead times shared at time of order.

04

Ship to You

Your finished Titan X robes — your colors, your name, your design — shipped directly to you or your team.

Team orders, club orders, and individual orders all welcome. We accommodate custom quantities — reach out and we'll discuss what works for you.

Not Just a Robe with Your Name On It

The Custom Doesn't Change the Core

Every custom Titan X robe — regardless of color or back design — uses the same three-layer electrospun nanofiber construction. The custom printing is applied to the outer nylon shell without compromising the nanofiber membrane beneath it. You get your colors and your identity, on a robe that is still the highest-performing wrestling robe available.
